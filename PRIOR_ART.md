# Prior art — Pose-Resolved EM Scanner / Rover

This comparison supports the accompanying [project description](README.md). It distinguishes earlier disclosures and demonstrations from the capabilities proposed for this project. An identical project title is not required for a technically relevant overlap, and failure to locate an identical complete system is not evidence of novelty.

## Instruments and research prototypes

- [Geometrics MetalMapper 2x2](https://www.geometrics.com/product/metalmapper-2x2/) and its [operations manual](https://www.geometrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/860-00158-01_B-MM2X2-Manual-Ver-2.2.pdf): multiple transmitter/receiver channels, dynamic and cued surveys, positioning and orientation metadata. A practical comparator for acquisition and calibration.
- [Man-Portable Vector sensor technical description and field evaluation](https://serdp-estcp.mil/projects/details/3697eda6-34ca-411d-8af4-b1e5d1e36862): 50-cm TX loop and five receivers measuring three field components; dynamic surveys and stationary measurements at multiple locations, interpreted jointly with accurate positioning. It is an important comparator for the proposed dimensions and workflow; it is not the proposed rotating three-RX carrier.
- [Geophex GEM-2 manual](https://geophex.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/GEM-2-Operators-Manual.pdf): multifrequency EMI and practical acquisition tradeoffs. Frequency diversity is an established instrument capability.
- [Robot-assisted soil apparent conductivity measurements, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05128): a mobile robot carrying an adjustable EMI sensor. Relevant to the carrier and sensor placement, not evidence of arbitrary metal-shape reconstruction.

Performance numbers from these systems apply to their respective targets, apparatus and conditions. They are not specifications for Pose-Resolved EM Scanner / Rover.

## Closest overlap with the three-RX refinement

The description of [US8125220B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8125220B2/en), in the passages on coil arrays and rotation before the figure descriptions, explicitly discusses parallel reception, symmetric arrays requiring less than a full rotation, examples dividing a full turn among receivers, and measurement after angular steps. Thus the general rationale for several RX coils on a rotating carrier is already disclosed. The earlier ring-equivalence calculation is a consistency check, not a new technical principle. The buried-target platform and proposed statistical workflow are not established as the same complete implementation. Description overlap must not be confused with satisfying all limitations of the granted claims.

## Additional significant application with an evidence gap

[US20260227541A1, Autonomous Rover for Detection of Precious Metals and Meteorites](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20260227541), published 6 August 2026, describes rover sensing, trained classification and stopped confirmation scans. The reproduced FIG. 9 discussion includes training on known materials, dimensions and burial depths. Independent claims 1, 11 and 18 respectively concern a hardware combination, an AI rover arrangement and staged AI decisions; all training details cannot be attributed to every independent claim.

Only the reproduced application text was obtained for this audit. The official publication PDF, family and authoritative legal status remain unverified. This is a significant overlap to follow up, not the sole basis of a novelty conclusion or proof of an identical probabilistic reconstruction.

## Scope of the proposed contribution

The project describes an intended integration of known EMI acquisition, measured geometry, reference learning and uncertainty treatment, with optional adaptive selection. Broad claims to the first use of these mechanisms, useful independent coil movement, or adaptive inductive scanning are not supported. No specific original technical mechanism has yet been established in the available project description. Any narrower technical contribution or measured advantage would have to be established for a concrete implementation against the closest relevant baselines, including existing adaptive EMI methods. Improvement over a nonadaptive survey alone would not establish an advance over this prior art.

A collection of overlapping sources does not automatically establish the legal outcome for every possible patent claim. Equally, not finding one document with every proposed detail does not establish novelty of their combination.
